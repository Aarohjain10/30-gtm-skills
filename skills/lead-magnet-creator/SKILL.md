# Lead Magnet Creator

## What this gets you
A free asset that the right people ask for, built from material you already own, with a plan to deliver it and a post to promote it. You give value first, and the people who raise a hand are the ones worth talking to.

## Before you start
Read `business-profile.md` if it exists (ideal customer, offer, proof). If not, ask once, max 6 questions:
1. Who do you want to attract (role, business type)?
2. What do you sell, and what is the first thing a buyer needs before they would buy?
3. What do you already have: documents, templates, prompts, checklists, spreadsheets, recorded calls, past answers to questions?
4. Where will you promote it (your own posts, newsletter, website)?
5. What can you deliver by hand today (email, message, a shared link)?
6. Anything you cannot share (client data, confidential material)?

## Steps
1. **Inventory.** List what the user already has. Remove anything with client or confidential data.
2. **Pick the asset.** Score each candidate 1 to 3 on: solves one specific problem for the target, usable in under 10 minutes, shows how you think, natural step toward your offer. Choose the highest. Prefer a list, template or prompt pack over a long guide.
3. **Name it with a count.** "[N] [things] to [outcome]". The count must equal the real number of items you will deliver. Use a [REAL N] placeholder until the content exists.
4. **Outline the contents.** One line per item or section, each framed as a benefit to the reader. Add a one page "start here".
5. **Set the gate.** The reader comments one keyword or replies to a message. No gate beyond that. Do not ask for more data than needed.
6. **Delivery path.** Comment keyword, then you send a message with the link or file. Do the sending yourself or with a tool the platform permits, and only to people who asked. Write the delivery message, include a line that they can say stop and you will not message again.
7. **Follow up once.** Three days later, one short message asking which item helped. No sequence, no pressure.
8. **Promo post.** Write the post (hand to `linkedin-post-writer` for the full voice process). Real proof in the opener, the named asset, one keyword.
9. **Measure.** Count comments, deliveries, replies, calls. Starting settings only; tune to your data.

## Output format
1. Chosen asset and why (scores table).
2. Name with count.
3. Contents outline.
4. Delivery path and the delivery message.
5. Follow up message.
6. Promo post draft.
7. Tracking sheet columns: date, person, keyword comment, delivered, replied, call booked.

## Worked example
Example scenario, not a customer. A fictional independent HR consultant, Lena Ortiz, helps 20 to 100 person companies with their first hiring process. Target: founders making their first five hires.

Inventory: a scorecard she uses for interviews, a list of questions she asks references, an offer letter template, 40 emails of candidate rejection wording, a call recording with a client (confidential, excluded).

Scores (1 to 3: specific problem, under 10 minutes, shows thinking, leads to offer):
| Candidate | Problem | 10 min | Thinking | Leads to offer | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Interview scorecard | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 12 |
| Reference questions | 3 | 3 | 2 | 2 | 10 |
| Offer letter template | 2 | 2 | 1 | 2 | 7 |
| Rejection wording | 2 | 3 | 1 | 1 | 7 |

Chosen: interview scorecard plus reference questions, combined as one pack.

Name: "The [REAL N] interview questions I use to hire a first employee" (N must equal the questions in the final scorecard, for example the actual count Lena puts in).

Contents:
1. A one page scorecard so every candidate is judged the same way
2. [REAL N] questions that show how someone works, not how they interview
3. What a good and a weak answer sounds like, for each
4. [REAL N] reference check questions that get honest answers
5. A start here page: how to use it in your next interview

Gate: comment SCORECARD.

Delivery message (DRAFT): "Hi [NAME], thanks for asking for the scorecard. Here it is: [REAL LINK]. Start with page one. If you would rather not hear from me again, say stop and I will not message you."

Follow up (3 days later, once): "Did you get a chance to use the scorecard? If one question was unclear, tell me and I will explain it."

Promo post (DRAFT):
"Hiring your first employee is a guess unless you ask everyone the same questions.
I wrote down the [REAL N] questions I use [REAL: Lena confirms she uses them with clients].
It is free. Includes what a good and weak answer sounds like.
What you get:
1. A fair way to compare candidates
2. Better questions in your next interview
3. References that tell you something
Comment SCORECARD and I will send it.
#hiring #startups #hr"

Tracking: log each comment, delivery and reply in the sheet. A reply that mentions hiring soon is a lead for a call.

## Rules
- Build from what the user really has. No invented assets, counts or results.
- The count in the name must match the real item count.
- Never include client confidential data.
- Deliver only to people who asked. One follow up, then stop. Honor stop requests.
- No guaranteed leads, meetings or revenue from the magnet.
- Platform terms apply; no automated comment or message tools that break them.
- No product or supplier names. No dashes. Max 3 hashtags in posts.
- Drafts need user approval.
