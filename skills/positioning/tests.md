# Tests for positioning
## Should trigger
1. "Write my 30 second pitch."
2. "Why should someone pick me over the other options?"
3. "How do I explain what I do without naming competitors?"
4. "Give me a positioning statement for my consulting firm."
## Should NOT trigger (and which skill instead)
1. "Write my offer and pricing" -> offer-builder
2. "Create messages for each persona and pain" -> messaging-matrix
3. "Answer this objection: you're too expensive" -> objection-replies
