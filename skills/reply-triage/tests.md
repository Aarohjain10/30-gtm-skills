# Tests for reply-triage

1. "Can you sort these 12 replies from my campaign and tell me what to do with each?"
2. "Triage my inbox, I have replies and I don't know which to answer first"
3. "What do I do when someone replies 'remove me' or 'not now'?"
## Should NOT trigger (and which skill instead)
1. "Write a reply to someone who says we're too expensive" -> objection-replies
2. "Help me book a call with the person who said yes" -> meeting-booking
