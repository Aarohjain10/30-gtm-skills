# 30 GTM Skills

30 free skills for AI agents that help you set up and run AI-assisted outbound and sales: work out what you sell and to whom, find the right people, write messages in your voice, handle replies, and measure what to change. Each skill does one job and hands you drafts to approve.

## What is in this repo

| Folder | What it is |
|---|---|
| `skills/` | The 30 skills. Neutral: they work with any tool, or with just pasted text and a CSV. |
| `sendora-companion/` | Optional guides for doing each of the 30 skills inside Sendora, with the exact steps and a prompt to paste. |
| `00-start-here/` | A one page quick start and a setup prompt. |

## Who it is for
- Founders who want more conversations with the right buyers without hiring first.
- Sales leads who want a repeatable process and better first drafts.
- Agencies and freelancers who want to sell an AI sales team to clients as a new revenue line.

## Install in 3 steps
1. Copy the folders inside `skills/` into your agent's skills folder (for Claude Code, `~/.claude/skills/`). Each skill is its own folder with a `SKILL.md`.
2. Open your agent.
3. Say "run gtm-router".

For a guided setup, paste the prompt from `00-start-here/setup-prompt.md`.

## The 30 skills, in the order to use them
**0-setup**
- gtm-router: tells you which skill to run next.
- business-interview: captures what you sell, to whom and what proof you have, as `business-profile.md`.

**1-offer**
- offer-builder: turns what you do into an offer people want.
- icp-builder: defines who to go after and who to skip.
- positioning: says why you, in a way buyers remember.
- messaging-matrix: matches the right message to each buyer type.
- proof-checker: keeps only claims you can really back.

**2-find-leads**
- competitor-engagers: finds people already engaging with your competitors.
- signal-finder: spots buying signals such as hiring or funding.
- list-quality-check: cleans a list before you use it.
- lead-scoring: ranks who to contact first.

**3-reach**
- cold-email: drafts first emails that sound human.
- follow-up-sequence: plans the follow-ups after the first email.
- channel-order: sets email, then text, then WhatsApp.
- text-whatsapp-compliance: checks you have the right to message.
- spam-check: flags wording that hurts delivery.
- deliverability-plan: plans safe sending volume and setup.
- inbound-voice-prompt: prepares an AI voice script for people who replied or asked.

**4-reply-and-book**
- reply-triage: sorts replies by what to do next.
- objection-replies: drafts calm answers to common pushback.
- meeting-booking: moves interested people to a booked time.
- discovery-call-prep: prepares you for the first call.

**5-content**
- linkedin-post-writer: drafts posts that attract the right buyers.
- lead-magnet-creator: designs something useful to give away.
- case-study-writer: turns real client results into a story.

**6-measure-and-grow**
- weekly-review: shows what to keep, cut or test.
- reactivation-play: wakes up past leads and customers.
- referral-outreach: asks happy customers for introductions.

**7-agency**
- agency-offer-packaging: builds a sellable package, price and pitch for clients.
- client-report: writes an honest monthly report from your real numbers.

## Using Sendora?
The skills do not need it. If you do use Sendora, open `sendora-companion/` for the steps to run each skill there.

## Honest limits
- Everything is a draft until you approve it. Nothing should be sent without your review.
- No skill guarantees meetings, replies, deliverability or revenue.
- It works with Claude plus a CSV or pasted text. If you have a live tool connected, Claude can use it, but that is optional.
- Texting and calling rules differ by place. Check local rules; the skills are not legal advice.
- LinkedIn and X are used only to find and understand people, never as outreach channels.

Our results. Yours depend on your list, offer and market.
