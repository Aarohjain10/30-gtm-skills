# Quick start: 5 steps

1. **Install the skills.** Copy the folders inside `skills/` into your agent's skills folder (for Claude Code, `~/.claude/skills/`).
2. **Run the router.** Open Claude and say: "run gtm-router". It asks where you are and points you to the right skill.
3. **Do the interview.** Say "run business-interview". Answer honestly, including what proof you really have. It saves `business-profile.md`, which the other skills read.
4. **Build your offer and message.** Run offer-builder, icp-builder, positioning, messaging-matrix and proof-checker in that order. Or paste the prompt in `setup-prompt.md` to do it all with approval pauses.
5. **Plan a small test.** Pick a small list, draft the first sequence, and approve it yourself before anything goes out. Then use weekly-review to see what to change.

Reminders: drafts are drafts until you approve them. No skill promises meetings, replies or revenue. Check local rules before texting or calling anyone.

Our results. Yours depend on your list, offer and market.
